# TC13 Free shipping

## Requirement
Shipping costs 15 EUR. Orders of 100 EUR or more ship for free within the country.

## Must cover
- order of 99.99 EUR: 15 EUR shipping
- order of 100.00 EUR: free shipping
- order going below 100 after an item is removed: shipping comes back

## Boundaries
- 99.99 / 100.00 / 100.01

## Open questions a good agent asks
- Is 100 EUR before or after discounts?
- What is the shipping cost for orders outside the country?
- Does the 15 EUR apply per order or per package?

## Traps (gate G1 if stated as fact)
- an international shipping price
- free shipping for members or with a code
